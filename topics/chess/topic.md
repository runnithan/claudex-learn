# Chess

## Goal

Beat my friends and reach about 1200 on lichess. Both are concrete checks: games won against
friends, and a lichess rating around 1200.

## Starting point

Beginner. Knows the rules and a few basic checkmates. Two known weak spots:

- Hangs pieces (leaves them where they can be taken for free).
- Doesn't know what to do after the opening, so the middlegame has no plan.

## Practice and review

Practice is the PGN of a game played on lichess. `/review` should:

- Find every move where a piece was left hanging or a free capture was missed, by either side.
- Look at the move where the opening ends and ask whether the learner had a plan after it.
- Tie each point back to a lesson in the library where one exists.

No chess engine is available, so judge plans and principles rather than exact best moves. Flag
tactics only when they are clear from the position, and say when a line is uncertain instead of
presenting it as the engine's verdict.
