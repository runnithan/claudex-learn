# Chess: learning path

Goal: Beat my friends and reach about 1200 on lichess.
Built 2026-09-24 from 50 lessons. Run /path chess again after new lessons land.

## Stage 1: Stop hanging pieces

Hanging pieces is your biggest leak, and tactics decide most games below master level, so a blunder check comes before any theory. **Milestone:** before every move you can name your opponent's threat and count attackers and defenders on the square you're moving to.

- [Make tactics your first study priority](lessons/thinking-process/prioritize-tactics-below-master-level.md)
- [Count material with the 1-3-3-5-9 piece values before trading](lessons/strategy/count-material-with-standard-piece-values.md)
- [Before each move, ask what your opponent wants to do](lessons/thinking-process/ask-what-your-opponent-wants-before-you-move.md)
- [Count attackers and defenders before you move a piece or pawn to a square](lessons/tactics/count-attackers-and-defenders-before-you-commit.md)
- [Keep your pieces defended even before they're attacked](lessons/tactics/keep-your-pieces-defended-before-they-are-attacked.md)
- [Don't count a pinned piece as a defender](lessons/tactics/a-piece-pinned-to-the-king-cannot-capture-or-guard.md)
- [When you see a good move, look for a better one](lessons/thinking-process/when-you-see-a-good-move-look-for-a-better-one.md)

## Stage 3: Finish the opening and meet your opponent's ideas

Once the first moves are sound, finish development cleanly and learn what to do when the opponent attacks early or grabs the center. **Milestone:** you can complete development (queen on the second rank, rooks connected) and answer an early attack or a big enemy pawn center.

- [Don't open the center while you're behind in development](lessons/openings/do-not-open-the-center-while-behind-in-development.md)
- [Answer a hasty attack with a counterstrike in the center](lessons/openings/answer-a-hasty-attack-with-a-central-counterstrike.md)
- [Control the center with pieces, not only with pawns](lessons/openings/control-the-center-with-pieces-not-only-pawns.md)

This is your second weak spot: with development done, these lessons give you something to do next. **Milestone:** when the opening ends you can pick a plan (gain central space, activate a benched piece, take an open file, keep the threats coming) and check your back rank before your rooks leave it.
