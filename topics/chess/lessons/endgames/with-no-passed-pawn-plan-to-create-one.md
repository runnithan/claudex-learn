# No passed pawn? Make creating one your plan

## TL;DR

In a quiet endgame with no obvious plan, pick the pawn most likely to become passed and work step by step to make it passed.

## Why it matters

The endgame video says quiet endgames without passed pawns confuse people the most, because there's no obvious target. One clear goal (turn this pawn into a passed pawn) makes every move easier to choose.

## How to apply

1. Look at each of your pawns and ask which has the best chance of becoming passed (in the video, a pawn with no enemy pawn on its file). 2. Work out what has to happen for it to become passed, such as pushing a neighboring pawn and then the pawn itself. 3. Before each push, make sure nothing hangs. In the video, White defends a pawn with the king before pushing its neighbor, brings the rook up so the push square is covered as many times as the opponent attacks it, and guards the rook when it gets attacked. 4. Once the trades leave you a passed pawn, keep repeating: control the next square, then push.

## Check yourself

You're in a rook endgame with no passed pawns and no obvious targets. One of your pawns has no enemy pawn on its file. Following this lesson, what's your plan, and what do you check before each push?
