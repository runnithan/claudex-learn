# Checkmate with two rooks (or queens) using the staircase

## TL;DR

Use one heavy piece to cut the king off along a rank or file, check with the other to push it back one line, and repeat until it's mated at the edge.

## Why it matters

The endgame video calls this a must-know pattern, and it works with two rooks, two queens, or a rook and a queen. Without a method, it's easy to give one check too many and lose a rook to the king.

## How to apply

1. Cut the king off with one rook along a file or rank. 2. Check with the other rook, so the two rooks control neighboring lines and the king has to step back. 3. Keep alternating until it's checkmate. If the king comes up and attacks a rook, don't give a check that hangs it: move that rook out of danger, as far from the king as possible, then continue the pattern. Place the rooks so they can keep checking without getting in each other's way. With queens it's even easier. Watch out for stalemate (see the next lesson).

## Check yourself

You're mating with two rooks. The enemy king steps next to the rook you were about to check with. What does this lesson say to do with that rook?
