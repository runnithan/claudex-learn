# Beat a pawn about to promote with zigzag queen checks

## TL;DR

With a queen against a pawn one step from promoting, give zigzag checks until the enemy king has to stand in front of its own pawn. Use the move that gains you to bring your king closer, and repeat.

## Why it matters

The endgame video: after a pawn race you may get a queen while the opponent's pawn is about to promote too. You should win, but only if you know the method.

## How to apply

1. Give checks that bring your queen closer to the pawn each time, zigzagging toward the enemy king. 2. When the king sidesteps, move the queen closer and attack the pawn. 3. Keep checking until the king's only way to keep the pawn is to step in front of it (any other king move drops the pawn). Now it blocks its own pawn. 4. Use that tempo to bring your king one step closer. 5. When the king steps off the pawn, start checking again. Once your king arrives, you usually win the pawn or give mate.

## Check yourself

You've just made a queen, but the enemy king is next to its pawn, which is one step from promoting, and your king is far away. What does this lesson tell you to aim for with your checks, and what do you do with the move that gains you?

## Caveat

Outside these sources: standard endgame theory holds that against a rook's pawn or a bishop's pawn on the seventh rank, the defending king can often use stalemate tricks to draw. So this method works reliably mainly against center and knight pawns. The video presents the method as a general win and doesn't mention these exceptions.
