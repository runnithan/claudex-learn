# When you're winning, check every move for stalemate

## TL;DR

Before any move that doesn't give check, make sure your opponent will still have a move. If they're not in check and have no move, it's stalemate: a draw.

## Why it matters

The endgame video shows that running the staircase mate 'unthinkingly' with two queens can end in stalemate. It also shows Magnus Carlsen, a pawn up in a totally winning ending, drawing because his opponent set a stalemate trap with Ka4: the only useful move, capturing on c4, left White stalemated. Even the strongest players fall for it.

## How to apply

When you're far ahead, especially against a lone king or a king whose pawns are blocked, check before each quiet move. After it, will your opponent be in check? If not, will they have any move at all? If they won't, choose a different move.

## Check yourself

You have a queen and king against a lone king in the corner. You find a quiet queen move that takes away every square from their king without giving check. What should you notice before playing it?
