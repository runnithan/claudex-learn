# Learn the typical middlegame plans that go with each opening you play

## TL;DR

Once the opening is over, you have to make plans from the features of the position, and the pawn structure largely shapes them. When you learn an opening, also learn the plans its usual pawn structure calls for.

## Why it matters

Wikipedia: the pawn structure is relatively static and largely decides the strategic nature of a position. In the middlegame, when opening theory has run out, players have to assess the position and form plans from its features. Typical plans, like the minority attack (attacking with queenside pawns against an opponent who has more pawns on that side), are often suited only to certain pawn structures that come from a particular group of openings. So opening study should be connected with the plans typical of the middlegames it leads to.

## How to apply

For each opening you play, note the pawn structure it usually leads to and find out the standard plan for that structure. In your own games, when the opening ends, look at the pawns first and ask what plan they suggest.
