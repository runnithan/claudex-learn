# Connect your rooks by clearing your back rank

## TL;DR

Finish development by clearing every piece from between your rooks. Connected rooks protect each other and can go to whichever file is best.

## Why it matters

The opening video explains that once nothing stands between the rooks, either rook can move to any square on the back rank, so you can choose the best file for each one. In its example, Black would put both rooks on semi-open files to press down them. Wikipedia adds that connected rooks protect each other, which reduces the risk of a back-rank skewer, where the king is checked and the rook behind it is captured.

## How to apply

After castling and developing the minor pieces, move the queen off the back rank (usually to a central second-rank square) so the rooks see each other. Then choose files for the rooks based on the position.

## Check yourself

You've castled and developed your knights and bishops, but your queen still sits between your rooks. What move does this lesson point toward, and what does it let your rooks do next?
