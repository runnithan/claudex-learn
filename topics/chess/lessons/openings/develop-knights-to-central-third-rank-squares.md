# Put your knights on the central squares of your third rank

## TL;DR

Develop knights to the central squares of your third rank (sixth rank as Black), such as f6, not to the edge or to e7. Only go elsewhere if that square is really unavailable.

## Why it matters

The opening video says players often put a knight on e7 and find a way to justify it, but on e7 or h6 the knight is less active. From f6, Black's knight presses on the center, supports key squares and in some lines is ready to jump to g4.

## How to apply

By default, send each knight to its central third-rank square (the video's example is f6 for Black's kingside knight). The exception: if that square has been taken away and you can't do anything about it, develop the knight somewhere else. The video's case is an enemy pawn on e5 that takes away f6.

## Check yourself

Playing Black, you're tempted to develop your kingside knight to e7 because it feels safer. The f6 square is free and no enemy pawn covers it. What does this lesson tell you to do?
