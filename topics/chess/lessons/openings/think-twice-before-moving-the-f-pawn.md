# Think twice before moving your f-pawn

## TL;DR

Pushing the f-pawn can support the center, but it opens a diagonal to your king. Make sure the gain is worth the exposure.

## Why it matters

The opening video treats the f-pawn separately from the other pawns. It can reinforce control of the center, but the obvious downside is that it exposes your king to attacks along the diagonal it used to block, and that can become dangerous.

## How to apply

Before pushing the f-pawn, look at the diagonal leading to your king: once the pawn has moved, can an enemy piece attack along it? Push only when the gain in the center is clear and your king won't be caught on that diagonal.

## Check yourself
