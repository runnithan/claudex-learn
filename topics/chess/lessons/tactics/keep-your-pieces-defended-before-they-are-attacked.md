# Keep your pieces defended even before they're attacked

## TL;DR

Don't wait for a threat before protecting a piece. And when you spot an undefended enemy piece, go after it right away.

## Why it matters

Wikipedia explains that if you only add a defender once a piece is attacked, it may cost you a tempo, or be impossible because of a fork or a discovered attack. Keeping pieces defended in advance heads off the opponent's future plans (Nimzowitsch called this 'overprotection'). The same logic works in reverse: undefended enemy pieces are weaknesses you should exploit immediately.

## How to apply

After each move, scan your pieces for any that have no defender, and prefer moves that leave everything protected. Also defend key squares, open files and your back rank, especially when your pawn structure is weak. Then scan the opponent's pieces for any without a defender and look for a way to attack them.

## Check yourself

Your knight sits on a central square with no defender, and nothing attacks it yet. You have a free move. What does this lesson suggest, and why not wait until the knight is attacked?
