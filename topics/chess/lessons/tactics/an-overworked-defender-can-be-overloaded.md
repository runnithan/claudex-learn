# Check whether a defender is doing two jobs at once

## TL;DR

A defended piece isn't automatically safe. If its defender is also guarding something else, that defender is overworked, and forcing it to do one job can drop the other.

## Why it matters

Wikipedia: if the defending piece is also defending something else, it is called an overworked piece and may not be able to do its job. In the thinking-process video, White threatens Ba3 to overload a black piece: once that piece is forced to capture, a black rook is lost.

## How to apply

For each enemy defender, ask what else it's guarding. If one piece guards two things, attack one of them or force the defender away, and the other becomes loose. The thinking-process video also looks for chances to 'remove the defender' of a key piece. Apply it to your own position too: don't leave one piece responsible for two jobs.

An enemy rook is the only defender of both their knight and their back rank. What does this lesson suggest you look for?
