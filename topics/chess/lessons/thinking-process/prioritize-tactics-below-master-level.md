# Make tactics your first study priority

## TL;DR

Below master level, games are decided by tactics (move-by-move threats and defenses) more often than by strategy, which is why many coaches treat studying tactics as the most efficient way to improve your results.

## Why it matters

Wikipedia: until players reach master level, tactics tend to decide the outcome of games more often than strategy, which is why many coaches stress tactics as the most efficient way to improve. The two can't be fully separated either: strategic goals are mostly achieved through tactics, and tactical chances grow out of earlier strategic play.

## How to apply

Give tactics the biggest share of your study time. Learn strategy too, since it sets up your tactical chances, but at this stage don't let it crowd out tactics.

## Check yourself

You have an hour of chess study this week and are choosing between a chapter on pawn structures and a set of tactics puzzles. Which does this lesson point you to, and why?
